## Brand & Style

This design system embodies high-tier industrial precision, pairing the technological clarity of premium EV manufacturers (Polestar, Tesla) with the rugged authority of heavy-duty transport giants (Scania, Facchini). 

The target audience comprises fleet managers, logistics directors, mining procurement leads, and corporate decision-makers evaluating zero-emission commercial fleets and heavy-payload solutions. The emotional response must radiate engineering competence, institutional trust, and forward-thinking sustainability.

Visual foundations rely on structured architectural layouts, precision metric displays, crisp light-reflecting metallic accents, and high-contrast deep executive canvases. Surfaces alternate between sterile product-showcase whites and commanding deep obsidian navy backdrops to balance technical scrutiny with prestige.

## Colors

The color palette anchors the experience in executive gravitas and engineered prestige:

- **Executive Navy (`#1B263B` / Pantone 534 C):** Primary interactive shade, primary CTA fills, header backgrounds, and corporate grounding surfaces.
- **Corporate Gold / Champagne (`#B39255` / Pantone 465 C):** Secondary accent reserved for high-value highlights, efficiency badges, premium vehicle trims, ROI calculators, and metallic accent lines.
- **Deep Carbon (`#0D1B2A` / Pantone Black 6 C):** Tertiary tone used for high-impact display headlines, solid technical footers, and inverted hero containers.
- **Neutral Platinum (`#E0E1DD` / Pantone Cool Gray 3 C):** Framing borders, data grid dividers, subtle technical cards, and secondary neutral backgrounds.
- **Pure White (`#FFFFFF`):** High-clarity primary canvas for vehicle configurators, data specification tables, and clean commercial reading areas.

Subtle linear gradients (`135deg, #C5A468 0%, #B39255 50%, #9E7D42 100%`) are utilized strictly on high-impact conversion buttons and certified seals to reproduce the champagne metallic sheen seen in automotive branding.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy implements a tripartite structure balancing engineering performance with executive readability:

- **Space Grotesk (Headlines & Display):** Imparts technical boldness, structured geometry, and automotive posture to vehicle classifications, load capacities, and strategic value propositions.
- **Inter (Body & Content):** Delivers clean readability for fleet analysis, leasing explanations, and regulatory compliance copy.
- **JetBrains Mono (Technical Metrics & Data):** Communicates exactitude for operational specifications (kWh capacity, torque rating, charging times, payload tonnage, and telemetry values).

Letter-spacing is intentionally drawn tighter across display levels to project density and authority, while technical labels leverage slight tracking expansion for instant scanning in high-density data sheets.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model utilizes a strict 12-column desktop grid with a maximum content container width of 1440px. The structural grid transitions to 8 columns on tablet devices and 4 columns on mobile viewports.

Spacing rhythm follows an 8px base grid logic. Technical specification matrices, comparison tables, and chassis schematics employ condensed internal spacing (`space-sm` to `space-md`) to present data compactly, while exterior showcase cards and hero headers deploy generous outer breathing room (`space-xl` to `margin`) to establish high-end industrial grandeur.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects exaggerated neomorphic or generic shadow treatments in favor of precision automotive surface transitions:

- **Tonal Layers:** Elevation is primarily established by transitioning from `#FFFFFF` base canvases to `#0D1B2A` cockpit layers or subtle `#E0E1DD` technical recess backgrounds.
- **Engineered Micro-Borders:** Panels and cards utilize fine 1px outlines (`#E0E1DD` in light mode; `rgba(224, 225, 221, 0.15)` over `#1B263B` backgrounds) to mimic machined body panels and chassis seams.
- **Directional Grounding Shadows:** Floating product cards and configurator toolbars receive a subtle, wide-angle shadow tinted with primary navy: `0 12px 32px -8px rgba(13, 27, 42, 0.08)`.
- **Metallic Hover Illumination:** Interactive cards receive a focused border glow transition into gold: `border-color: #B39255; box-shadow: 0 0 20px -4px rgba(179, 146, 85, 0.25)`.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a soft, architectural corner geometry (`roundedness: 1`). 

Buttons, data chips, inputs, and specification cards employ a crisp 4px (`0.25rem`) border radius, with larger display containers maxing out at 8px (`0.5rem`). This avoids hyper-circular consumer aesthetics, reflecting structural steel, heavy implements, and the sharp industrial silhouettes of commercial fleet vehicles.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Executive Fleet CTA):** Background `#1B263B`, text `#FFFFFF`, 4px radius, 48px height, medium tracking. Hover shifts to `#0D1B2A` with a subtle bottom accent line in `#B39255`.
- **Prestige / Quote CTA:** Metallic gradient background (`135deg, #C5A468 0%, #B39255 100%`), text `#0D1B2A`, bold weight. Focused for "Cotizar Unidad" and high-intent contact actions.
- **Secondary / Technical:** Transparent background with 1px border in `#1B263B` or `#E0E1DD`, text `#1B263B`.

### Technical Spec Chips & Badges
- **Metric Badges:** Height 26px, padding `0 10px`, typography `label-spec`. Features a light background tint (`rgba(27, 38, 59, 0.06)`) with `#1B263B` text and a left-aligned 2px indicator in `#B39255` (e.g., "100% ELÉCTRICO", "CARGA ÚTIL: 32T").
- **Status Indicators:** Pill dots with solid fills displaying charge rate, battery chemistry, or immediate stock availability.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Fields:** 48px height, 1px border in `#E0E1DD`, background `#FFFFFF`. Active focus transitions to a 1.5px border in `#1B263B` with a 2px offset gold ring (`rgba(179, 146, 85, 0.2)`).
- **Labels:** Set in `Inter` 13px weight 500, uppercase with 0.05em tracking in `#1B263B`.

### Product & Implement Cards (Facchini / JMEV / RODO)
- **Structure:** Clean 1px `#E0E1DD` border, `#FFFFFF` interior, 4px corner radius.
- **Data Ribbon:** Sub-grid containing 3 distinct columns showing: Autonomía (km), Batería (kWh), Capacidad (Kg) set in `label-metric` with `label-spec` descriptor labels.
- **Dark Mode Card Variant:** Background `#1B263B`, borders `rgba(224, 225, 221, 0.12)`, primary metric labels `#B39255`.

### Checkboxes & Segmented Controls
- **Controls:** Square-cut 18px indicators with 2px radius. Selected state fills with `#1B263B` featuring an SVG checkmark in `#B39255`.
- **Vehicle Segment Tabs:** Horizontal rail segmented buttons with dark navy indicator pills that highlight active chassis types (Semirremolques, Volquetes, Furgones Eléctricos).
